I'm Katja from Finland and I am somewhat tired of social media.

This blog is a social media free (hence no commenting either, send me an email or ping me on LinkedIn if you so wish) place for me to write down things that I learn, find interesting, find irritating, or just want to write about for some other reason :shrug:

This aims to be a diary-like place for thoughts that are not necessary worthy to post in other places.
Depending on the mood, some might be in Finnish but majority will probably be in English. Aika näyttää!

I work as a software developer (fullstack I guess one could call it), but I don't only like to code, I like to create usable and pretty software.
Bad UX makes me super angry and super frustrated because I feel it is so easy to avoid.
