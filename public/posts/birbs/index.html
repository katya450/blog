Happiness = trees + birbs

What happened in my brain when the goal failed?

After my totally catastrophic failure in the tech interview for my “dream job”, it forced me to think whether I was unprepared for it somehow on purpose? Well not knowingly, it was a failure from my there’s no arguing that, but there was someone knocking on the back of my head that this is not the thing anymore. It was The Thing when I used to live a walking distance from the job and I had less things to worry about in my everyday life. But nowadays, it would have required me to use public transportation for almost two hours every day. I hate using public transportation when it’s the only option. It would’ve made my working days 10 hours or more, instead of current 8ish. Sure the salary would’ve been slightly better. The domain would’ve been super interesting. But it would have taken me hours from the things that now bring me great, great joy: birds in my yard. Was my dream job just some noise from the years back?

I know it probably sounds stupid, comparing a job and the birds outside. But it is what it really comes down to, when I compare things and weigh the value on them.

Would working for a defence sector give me more joy, than having the option to watch birds playing in the trees outside my window? No.
Would working for a defence sector dependless on the language give me more joy, than having a normal job to develop software with Clojure? Also no.
Would working for a defence sector using Clojure give me more joy, than having the option to work regarless of time and place, also with Clojure? Definitely no.

Does it not mean, that my definition of a dream job has changed?
Would I have realized this without failing?

The big Q now is to find out what my next goal is.

To be honest.
There are some goals in my mind.
But.
But the very reason I chose this current job, was the dream of being still for a moment. To spend enough time with one system to get good in it. So that each day would not require excess struggle and feelings of unknown. To deepen my skills with Clojure.

So to be really honest I should really stick to that one goal instead of bouncing around like a madball.
