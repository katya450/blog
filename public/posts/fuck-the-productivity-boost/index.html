Fuck the productivity boost

Did you know that using AI increases your productivity by so so many percents? The more you use AI the more productive you are! And everything is and should be about productivity, right?! The more code you push push push out the more producive you are and the more value to the stakeholders! Value! Money! More agents! More!

Fuck that. FUCK. THAT. SHIT.

The more I see AI generated code, the more I see bugs. Bugs that are already on a PR, meaning it might look good in the code, but it breaks something else in the system. Features that work but are not even close to what the designer drew. Features that look ugly. Features with bad translations. Features that feel like no one bothered to look befofe they were in the production. It’s like productivity is more important than quality and it really hurts me.

So many companies are also declared themselves to be “AI first”. What does it mean then? It means AI is the first thing to think about, the first to implement, the first to do the job. Few companies are “Quality first” anymore. Why would anyone want to declare themselves being AI over quality? AI over people. AI over users? AI over usability? When declaring something as “FIRST”, everything else inevitably comes after that.

I have became tester, finder of the bugs.

Yea I’m not saying using AI at work is bad. I use it a lot, as a tool. It’s the idea of being faster, faster, giving more, more with less time. What are we in such a hurry running for? Let’s say we could clear our whole backlog (it’s a long one mind you) in two months? We would end up having features that were designed long ago and might not be valid anymore.

Shouldn’t this speed in writing code give us more time to plan features properly instead of just implementing each idea without giving them a decent design and evaluation? I deeply dispise the saying “Less is more” (I have a history with that saying) but I feel it would fit in today’s speedrunning world well.
